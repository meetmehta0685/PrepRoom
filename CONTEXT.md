# PrepRoom interviews

PrepRoom helps candidates practise software-engineering interviews with either an AI interviewer or another person.

## Language

**Interview session**:
A single practice attempt owned by one candidate and conducted by either an AI interviewer or a peer interviewer.
_Avoid_: Meeting, call, room

**Candidate**:
The person whose interview performance is being practised and evaluated.
_Avoid_: User, participant

**Interviewer type**:
The source of the questions in an interview session. It is either AI or peer.
_Avoid_: Mode, host type

**Peer interviewer**:
A person invited by the candidate to conduct the interview.
_Avoid_: Host, friend

**Interview track**:
The software-engineering subject that determines the interview questions.
_Avoid_: Category, topic

**Interview report**:
The completed evaluation of a candidate's answers in an AI interview session.
_Avoid_: Result, summary
